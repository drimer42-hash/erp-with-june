Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

The short version: ERP with June does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What you store

Any information you enter — your list of compulsions, daily plans, exposure logs, distress ratings, and notes — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party. We have no ability to access it.

Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we never receive it.

Device backups

If you use Apple's device backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup), your app data may be included in that backup under Apple's control and privacy terms. We do not have access to those backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries within the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

ERP with June is a self-help tool, not a medical device. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, contact a licensed clinician or your local emergency services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this URL.
